# Marketing Platform Policy

*Last updated: 24 September 2026*

## 1. About This Policy

This Policy applies to Clients using the email campaign feature of Loyalties Marketing (the "Marketing Platform"). The Marketing Platform includes the Campaigns page, the campaign editor, templates, test sends, scheduling, tracking and reporting, and AI-assisted features when they are available.

It builds on our Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy ("AUP") and adds detail specific to how the Marketing Platform works. Read it together with the AUP, our Promotions and Prizes Policy, and our Terms of Use. If this Policy conflicts with another document on a Marketing Platform matter, this Policy governs.

Words such as Client, Member, and Campaign have the same meaning as in the AUP.

## 2. Access, Plans and Refunds

Sending Campaigns is a paid feature. See our Terms of Use for current plan details, pricing, and GST.

A paid plan gives you access to the tools. It does not exempt you from this Policy, the AUP, or the Promotions and Prizes Policy. We may restrict or suspend sending for a breach of any of them, whatever your plan or payment status.

If your plan lapses or is downgraded:

- Scheduled Campaigns will not send.
- Your drafts, saved templates, and past reports stay in your account so you can pick up where you left off.

Your plan may set limits on sending volume and frequency.

**Refunds.** We decide refund requests case by case. Any refund we give is for the full amount you paid, including GST. Nothing in this Policy limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including your remedies if the service has a major failure.

- If we suspend or restrict your sending because you breached a policy, we don't automatically refund the unused period. You can still ask, and we will consider it.
- If we suspend or restrict your sending and it turns out you did not breach a policy, or the problem was on our side, we will refund or credit the affected period.

## 3. Who You Can Email

Campaigns go only to confirmed Members who have opted in to marketing from your venue.

- Members enter your list only by joining through your join page. There is no import, upload, or manual add. See Section 8 of the AUP.
- Before every send, including scheduled sends, we check that each recipient is still confirmed and has not unsubscribed, bounced, or complained. Anyone who has is left out. You can't override this.
- Each Member's consent covers your venue only. If you run more than one venue, each venue's Members consent to that venue, unless your join page told them clearly that the marketing covers a wider group.

## 4. How the Campaign Editor Works

Every Campaign follows a fixed structure that we set. It has a header, a hero, promotional text and an offer, a call-to-action button, and a required terms and footer section. You fill in the content. You can't change the structure.

You control:

- your text, including the headline, promotional text, offer, button text, and button link
- your images
- your logo and accent colour, which come from your card design
- a font, chosen from our curated list
- a light or dark theme

You can start a Campaign from one of our templates, from one of your saved templates, or by duplicating a past Campaign. However you start, this Policy applies to the finished Campaign.

The Campaigns page lists your Drafts, Scheduled Campaigns, and Sent Campaigns, with quick engagement numbers. The editor saves your drafts automatically, but keep your own copy of anything important. We don't guarantee that drafts will never be lost.

## 5. The Required Footer

Every Campaign automatically includes a footer. It is on by default and isn't optional. It contains:

- your business name and postal address, taken from your account details
- your terms or legal information for the Campaign
- a working unsubscribe link
- a "Report this email" link
- a short reminder of why the recipient is receiving the email, because they opted in through your join page
- a disclaimer that the email was sent using the Loyalties Marketing engine

**Your address.** It must be real and kept current. A street address or PO box is fine. If you work from home and don't want to show your home address, use a PO box or your registered business address.

**Legibility.** You can style the footer to match your branding. You must not remove, hide, shrink, or blend into the background any required information, or make it harder to read than the rest of the email. This applies in both the light and dark themes. We check footer size and contrast automatically. We may adjust the styling or block a Campaign that fails.

The footer helps you meet the Spam Act and CAN-SPAM. It does not make a Campaign compliant on its own. You are still responsible for who you send to and what you say.

## 6. Don't Change the Email Structure

You are free to write your own copy, choose your own images, and fill in the fields the editor gives you. You must not:

- edit a Campaign outside the editor, including by injecting raw HTML or code
- remove, disable, reorder, or move the footer so it is no longer the final element of the email
- add content anywhere in the email designed to look like the footer, an unsubscribe link, a report link, or a legal disclaimer when it isn't
- structure a Campaign to mislead a recipient about who sent it, why, or how to opt out

See Sections 9.6 and 10 of the AUP, which cover the same restriction from the technical side.

## 7. Content Standards for Campaigns

Campaigns should be friendly and promote your business honestly. Your text, images, subject line, and preview text must not:

- make false or misleading claims about your products, prices, availability, or offers
- use fake urgency, such as a false countdown or "only 3 left" when that isn't true
- use fake reviews or fake testimonials
- disparage a competitor or make misleading comparisons
- harass, threaten, or abuse a recipient
- mislead recipients about your affiliation with another business
- promote illegal goods, services, or activity, or anything not allowed under Section 6 of the AUP
- contain hate speech, discriminatory content, or sexually explicit material
- name, show, or describe a Member without their permission, for example a "customer of the month"

You must also:

- state the key terms of each offer in the terms section, including dates, limits, exclusions, and how to redeem it
- honour every offer you send
- follow the alcohol, health, and food rules in Section 6 of the AUP
- follow our Promotions and Prizes Policy for any draw, competition, or giveaway, and put the required promotion terms in the terms section

This list works alongside the fuller rules in the AUP, not instead of them.

## 8. Images, Fonts and Uploaded Media

- You must own or have a licence for every image, logo, font, and piece of media you use.
- Photos of identifiable people need their permission. Don't use photos of children without a parent's or guardian's permission.
- Don't use another business's logo unless you are entitled to.
- Keep real text in your email. An email made of one big image often lands in spam and can't be read when images are off. Put required information in text, never only inside an image. We may block a Campaign that is mostly image.
- Add alt text where the editor offers it.
- Fonts come from a curated list that works across email apps. We don't guarantee how a font displays in every email app.
- We may remove, resize, compress, or block any media, including for file size or safety reasons.

## 9. Test Sends, Scheduling and Sending

**Test sends.** Use test sends to check a Campaign before it goes out. Send them only to addresses you control, such as your own and your staff's. Don't send test emails to Members or anyone else, and don't use test sends to send promotional email. We may limit how many test emails you can send.

**Scheduling.** You choose when a Campaign sends. You are responsible for making sure the content is still accurate at that time, including offer dates. We re-check every recipient at send time (Section 3). If your plan lapses, or we suspend your account or your sending, scheduled Campaigns will not send. We may delay or throttle a send for deliverability or safety reasons.

**Sender.** Campaigns are sent from our sending domain under your venue's name.

**Replies.** Members can reply to a Campaign. Replies go to the contact email on your account. You must watch that inbox and respond within 5 business days. Treat any request to stop receiving emails as an unsubscribe and tell us at support@loyalties.marketing so we can action it straight away.

**Limits.** We may set limits on volume and frequency, including the number of Campaigns per week and per Member. New Clients may have lower limits at first.

**Deliverability.** All Clients share our sending domain. The complaint and bounce levels in Section 16 of the AUP apply to you.

## 10. Tracking and Reporting

**What we track.** We track opens using a tiny image in each email, clicks on links, which go through a redirect on our domain, bounces, unsubscribes, and spam complaints. We also track stamps earned after a Campaign, and clicks on the promo link on your card page.

**What you see.** Reports show results for each Campaign and may show the activity of individual Members. Open counts are unreliable because some email apps load images automatically, so treat them as a guide only. Click and stamp figures are a more reliable signal of real engagement.

**Your duties.** Members' engagement data is personal information. Use it only to run and improve your program and Campaigns. Don't sell or share it, upload it to advertising platforms, or use it to treat Members unfairly. Tell Members in your own privacy notice or on your join page that your emails use tracking, where the law requires.

**Our use.** We use tracking data to run the Marketing Platform, report to you, prevent abuse, and improve the Platform, including in aggregated and de-identified form. Our Privacy Policy gives more detail.

**Your results are private.** Your reports and your best-performing view are visible only to your account. We won't show your Campaigns or results to other Clients or publish them without your permission.

## 11. AI-Assisted Features

*This section applies once these features are available.*

AI features are optional. They may suggest copy or recommend a template. If you use them:

- AI can be wrong. Read and edit everything before you send it.
- You are responsible for the accuracy of every offer, price, and claim, including legal claims. AI-generated content is held to the same standards as content you write yourself.
- Don't enter Members' names, email addresses, or other personal information into prompts.
- We may use third-party AI providers to process your prompts. Our Privacy Policy lists them.
- Suggestions may resemble content other Clients receive. We don't promise originality.
- We may limit or turn off AI features at any time.

## 12. Templates and Ownership

We own the template system and our system templates. Your content stays yours. A template you save is a copy of your content in our structure.

We may update or retire system templates, and we may change the structure, to keep Campaigns compliant with the law or to fix problems. If we do, we will update your saved templates where we can, and tell you if a change affects how they look.

## 13. Enforcement

A breach of this Policy is handled the way the AUP handles breaches. See Section 17 of the AUP for the severity table and the fair process we follow.

Because Campaign sending is a paid feature, a serious or repeated breach can mean losing access to it specifically, even while the rest of your account stays active. We may also:

- block or hold a Campaign
- remove content
- pause scheduled Campaigns
- remove a saved template
- put stricter limits on your sending, such as a review before each send

For refunds after enforcement, see Section 2.

## 14. Reporting a Concern

Members, and anyone who has received a Campaign through Loyalties Marketing, can report a concern at **loyalties.marketing/abuse**. Every Campaign includes a "Report this email" link in its footer that opens the form with the Campaign already filled in. We follow up from support@loyalties.marketing. See Sections 14 and 15 of the AUP for what happens next.

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date. For material changes, we will email Clients at least 14 days before the change takes effect, unless we need to act sooner to comply with the law or to protect Members.

We may also add, change, or remove Marketing Platform features. We will try to give reasonable notice of changes that materially reduce functionality you actively use.
