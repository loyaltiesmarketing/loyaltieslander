# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 September 2026*

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Loyalties Marketing (ABN 35 140 467 638) ("Loyalties Marketing", "we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information through our loyalty program platform.

We're based at 453 Murray Road, Preston, Vic 3072, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and we take the same care with personal information about people in the United States.

## 2. Scope of This Policy and Who Is Responsible

This policy covers two groups of people:

- **Clients**, the businesses that create a dashboard account to run a loyalty program
- **Members**, the individuals who join a Client's loyalty program as a customer

Where something applies to only one group, we've said so. Capitalised words such as Campaign and Service Message have the meaning they have in our Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy.

**Who is responsible for what.** When you join a venue's loyalty program, two parties handle your information:

- **The venue (the Client).** It runs its own program and decides how to use your information for that program, within our Terms of Use and Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy. The venue is responsible for its own handling of your information and may have its own privacy notice. Please read it.
- **Loyalties Marketing.** We run the platform. We handle your information for the venue to operate the platform, and we are responsible for the information we collect for our own purposes, such as consent records, security logs, and abuse reports.

## 4. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information directly from you, through the join form, dashboard, manage page, Campaign tools, reports, and support conversations. We also collect some information automatically, as described in Sections 3.4 and 3.5.

Members join a venue's program only by signing up themselves. Clients can't import or upload contact lists into the Platform. We don't buy personal information from third parties.

## 5. Why We Collect and Use Information

**Service delivery.** Operating loyalty stamp programs and membership management, sending one-time login codes and confirmation emails, and sending marketing emails only to Members who have opted in and confirmed.

**Consent evidence.** Keeping a record of when, where, and how each Member agreed to marketing, so that we and the venue can show consent when needed.

**Campaign reporting.** Showing Clients how their Campaigns performed.

**Service improvement.** Understanding how the Platform is used so we can improve reliability, usability, and email deliverability. We may use aggregated and de-identified information for this and for statistics.

**Abuse, fraud, and security.** Monitoring for suspicious sending patterns, spam complaints, and other signs of misuse, so we can act under our Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy. We also use it to protect accounts and systems.

**Handling reports.** Reviewing and responding to reports and complaints.

**Billing and support.** Managing Client plans and responding to support requests.

**AI features.** Providing AI-assisted features to Clients who choose to use them, once they are available.

**Legal compliance.** Meeting our obligations under Australian and US law, including responding to lawful requests from authorities.

## 6. Marketing Communications and Your Consent

When you join a loyalty program, you're shown a checkbox to receive marketing emails from that venue. It is unticked by default. Ticking it is your choice. It is never a condition of joining, earning stamps, or getting a reward.

If you tick it, we send you a confirmation email. You won't receive marketing until you confirm. If you don't confirm, you stay in the program and receive no marketing. We record when, where, and under which wording you agreed.

You can withdraw consent at any time by:

- using the one-click unsubscribe link in any marketing email
- turning off the marketing toggle on your manage page

Withdrawing stops marketing emails from that venue. It doesn't affect any other venue you've joined. We still send Service Messages needed to run your membership, such as one-time login codes and notices about your card or the program, whatever your marketing preference. We keep an encoded record of your unsubscribe so it continues to be honoured.

If you receive a confirmation email or a marketing email you didn't ask for, someone may have entered your email address without your permission. Report it at loyalties.marketing/abuse and we will remove your address from that venue's list and block it from receiving marketing from that venue.

## 8. Overseas Disclosure

We host our data in Australia (Sydney). Some of our service providers, including email delivery and mapping providers, may process data on servers in the United States.

Under Australian Privacy Principle 8, before we disclose your information to an overseas recipient we take reasonable steps to make sure they don't handle it in a way that would breach the APPs, including through the contract terms we put in place with our providers.

## 9. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold, including:

- storing passwords as salted hashes through our authentication provider, not as plain text, so they aren't readable by our staff
- storing sensitive fields such as one-time login codes as hashed values rather than in their original form
- encrypting data in transit between your browser and our servers
- limiting internal access to personal information to what's needed for support and operations

We rely on our infrastructure providers, including Supabase, Resend, and Mapbox, for the security of the underlying hosting and delivery infrastructure, and we choose providers who publish their own security practices. No system is completely secure, and we can't guarantee that information will never be accessed, disclosed, or lost through circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

## 10. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy or as the law requires. Our current periods are:

| Information | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Client account and billing records | For the life of the account and 5 years afterward, to meet legal and accounting obligations |
| Member records (name, email, stamp history) | Until you remove your membership, then deleted or de-identified within 30 days |
| Consent records | 2 years after the membership ends, so we can show that consent was given |
| Encoded unsubscribe and bounce records | For as long as needed to honour opt-outs and block bad addresses |
| Campaign engagement records (opens, clicks, and similar) | Up to 24 months, then deleted or aggregated |
| Reports and support messages | 2 years after they are resolved |
| Security and server logs | 90 days |

We may keep information longer where the law requires it, or where we need it to deal with a dispute or a legal claim.

## 11. Access, Correction, and Deletion

You can view your current loyalty card details and update your name, email, or marketing preference at any time through the manage page.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information beyond what the manage page allows, contact us at privacy@loyalties.marketing. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will aim to respond within 30 days. We don't currently have an automated self-serve export tool, so we handle these requests manually.

**Deleting your information.** When you remove your membership on the manage page, we delete or de-identify your Member record within 30 days. We keep only:

- consent records for the period in Section 10
- an encoded (hashed) version of your email address linked to that venue, so we can honour your unsubscribe and block addresses that have bounced or complained
- anything the law requires us to keep

Removing your membership only affects that venue. To remove your information from every venue, remove each membership, or contact us at privacy@loyalties.marketing.

**Copies held by venues.** If a venue has exported information from its dashboard, we can't delete that copy. Venues must handle it as our Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy requires. Ask the venue directly to delete it.

**If your concern is about a venue's program, not your data.** If you have a complaint about a reward, offer, or program rule set by a specific venue, that's the venue's decision to make. Contact them directly. You can also report it at loyalties.marketing/abuse. If your concern is about how we handle your personal information, contact us at privacy@loyalties.marketing.

## 12. Cookies, Tracking, and Similar Technologies

**Cookies.** Clients and Members who sign in with a one-time code get a session cookie so the Platform can keep you signed in for that session.

**Email tracking.** Marketing emails include a tiny tracking image and tracked links so Clients can see open and click activity. Links in our emails go through a redirect on loyalties.marketing before they reach the destination, so we can record the click. This tracking is linked to you as an individual. Some email apps load images automatically, so open records are only a rough guide.

You can avoid tracking by not opening the email, blocking images in your email app, or not clicking links. You can also unsubscribe at any time (Section 6).

**No advertising or analytics.** We don't currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies, and we don't track you across other websites. If that changes, we'll update this policy and add appropriate consent controls.

**Do Not Track.** Because we don't track you across other websites, we don't change how the Platform works in response to Do Not Track signals.
