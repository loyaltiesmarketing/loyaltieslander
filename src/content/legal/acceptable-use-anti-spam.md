# Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy

*Last updated: 24 September 2026*

## 1. About This Policy

This Acceptable Use and Anti-Spam Policy ("Policy") sets the content and conduct rules for everyone using Loyalties Marketing. Loyalties Marketing (ABN 35 140 467 638) ("we", "us", "our") operates the Platform.

This Policy is part of our Terms of Use. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, Marketing Platform Policy, and Promotions and Prizes Policy. If this Policy conflicts with the Terms of Use on a specific point, this Policy governs on that point because it is the more specific document.

Where we say "must", it is a requirement. Where we say "we may", we have the right to act but no duty to.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Client | A business running a loyalty program on Loyalties Marketing |
| Member | An individual who joins a Client's loyalty program |
| Platform | The Loyalties Marketing software, website, dashboard, and related services |
| Campaign | An email sent to Members through the Marketing Platform |
| Marketing Email | Any email that promotes a Client's business, offers, or events, including every Campaign |
| Service Message | An email needed to run a Member's card, such as a one-time login code or a confirmation email |

## 2. Who This Applies To

This Policy applies to:

- **Clients**, including their staff, contractors, and agents. You are responsible for what they do on your account.
- **Members**, for their conduct on the Platform.
- **Anyone** who receives a Campaign or wants to report a concern. You do not need to be a Member to report.

## 3. Where You Can Use the Platform

The Platform is built for Clients operating in Australia and the United States, and for Members who join through those Clients. Marketing, privacy, and promotion laws differ by country.

Don't use the Platform to run a program or send marketing aimed at people in other countries, including the UK, the EU, Canada, and New Zealand, unless we have agreed in writing. If your Members are in both Australia and the United States, you must follow the law in both.

## 4. Age Rules

Members must be 18 or over. The join page tells Members this, and by joining they confirm it.

Clients must not:

- knowingly enrol anyone under 18
- encourage or help anyone under 18 to join
- design offers, images, or messages aimed at children

If you learn a Member is under 18, stop all marketing to them and tell us at support@loyalties.marketing. If we learn it, we may remove the membership and delete or de-identify the Member's information as our Privacy Policy describes.

Clients must be 18 or over and able to form a legally binding contract. If you sell age-restricted products, the age rules for those products apply as well. This Policy does not replace them.

## 5. Content and Program Standards for Clients

Your branding, join page, offers, reward descriptions, Campaigns, and any other content you put on the Platform ("Client Content") must not:

- contain hate speech, discriminatory content, or material that promotes or glorifies violence against a person or group
- threaten, harass, bully, or defame anyone, or publish someone's private information without permission
- contain sexually explicit, pornographic, or other adult content
- involve or sexualise children in any way. We report this to the authorities immediately
- promote or glorify self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, terrorism, or extremist groups
- promote illegal goods or services, or encourage dangerous or unlawful activity
- be false, misleading, or deceptive about your business, offers, prices, or how your loyalty program works. This includes fake reviews, fake testimonials, fake countdowns, and fake scarcity
- claim an endorsement or affiliation you don't have
- infringe someone else's intellectual property, privacy, or other rights (see Section 13)
- misrepresent how the Platform works, including NFC stamping or digital wallet passes
- promote pyramid schemes, get-rich-quick schemes, or multi-level marketing recruitment
- contain malware or phishing, or link to either

## 6. Restricted and Regulated Offers

Some industries are regulated. If your business or offer falls in one of these areas, extra rules apply.

**Alcohol.** You may promote alcohol only if you hold the licences your state or territory requires. Follow the liquor laws that apply to you, including rules on irresponsible promotions, and the ABAC Code. Don't offer alcohol rewards or promotions that encourage rapid or excessive drinking.

**Health, wellness, and cosmetic services.** Don't advertise prescription-only medicines to the public, including cosmetic injectables. Don't claim to treat, cure, or prevent a medical condition unless the law allows you to and you follow the therapeutic goods and health practitioner advertising rules that apply.

**Food and dietary claims.** Don't make allergen, dietary, or health claims that aren't accurate. This includes claims such as "gluten free" or "vegan".

**Not allowed on the Platform.** You must not promote tobacco, vaping products, cannabis products, gambling or betting, adult services, weapons, illegal drugs, lottery tickets, or cryptocurrency, investment, or credit products.

If your business is in a regulated category, or you're unsure, contact us before you send marketing. We may decline or restrict any business or category at our discretion.

## 7. Loyalty Program Standards

A loyalty program is a promise to your customers. Keep it. You must:

- publish your program rules where Members can see them, including how stamps are earned, what the reward is, any expiry, and any exclusions
- honour every reward a Member has earned under those rules
- state any expiry on stamps or rewards clearly before Members earn them
- give at least 30 days' notice before you make a change that reduces the value of stamps or rewards Members have already earned, and let them redeem under the old rules during that time
- give at least 30 days' notice before you close your program, and let Members redeem earned rewards in that time
- keep your NFC devices secure

You must not:

- charge Members to join or to earn stamps
- add, remove, or block stamps to defraud a Member, to punish them unfairly, or to discriminate
- refuse a reward because of a person's race, sex, disability, religion, or another attribute protected by anti-discrimination law

Disputes about rewards are between you and your Member. We don't decide them, but Members can report them under Section 14, and we may act if you keep breaching this Policy.

## 8. How Members Join and Consent

### 8.1 Members join themselves

Members enter your list only by signing up through your join page. There is no import, upload, or manual add feature, and you must not try to get around this. Members must type in their own details. Staff may help someone use the join page but must not enter details for them.

You must not:

- sign someone up with details they didn't enter themselves
- register other people's email addresses on a counter tablet, shared device, or your own device
- create fake or test Members to inflate your list
- use bots, scripts, or bulk tools on the join page
- ask customers to hand you their details so you can enter them

### 8.2 Marketing consent is separate and optional

The join page has a marketing checkbox that is unticked by default. Marketing consent is never a condition of joining, earning stamps, or getting a reward. Don't say or suggest otherwise. Don't change or work around how the join page asks for consent.

### 8.3 Members must confirm

After a Member opts in to marketing, we send a confirmation email. No Marketing Email goes to that Member until they confirm. A Member who doesn't confirm stays in your program but never receives Campaigns. We record when, where, and under which wording each Member consented.

### 8.4 Consent belongs to your venue

A Member's consent covers your business only. You must not:

- send promotions for other businesses
- sell, rent, share, or swap Members' details
- use Members' details to promote another business, including a related business you own, unless the join page told Members clearly that the marketing covers that group

### 8.5 Consent can be withdrawn

A Member can withdraw marketing consent at any time. After that, you must not email them. Only the Member can opt back in, by joining or updating their preference themselves.

### 8.6 If someone used my email address

If you receive a confirmation email or Campaign you didn't ask for, report it under Section 14. We will remove the address from that venue's list and block it from receiving marketing from that venue.

## 9. Marketing Email and Anti-Spam Standards

We design and control the email templates used for Campaigns. Sender identification, the unsubscribe link, the report link, and our contact details are built into every Campaign. You can't remove them. So your duties are mainly about what you write and how you use the tools.

### 9.1 Your duties

You must:

- send Campaigns only to confirmed Members
- keep your business name, logo, offer text, and other fields accurate and not misleading
- make subject lines and preview text match the content
- honour every offer you send
- use the templates and campaign tools as provided
- respond to Members who reply to your Campaigns
- not send a Campaign that is broken or malformed, whether on purpose or through misuse of the tools

### 9.2 Consent standards, Australia and the US

| | Australia (Spam Act 2003) | United States (CAN-SPAM Act) |
|---|---|---|
| Consent | Required before you send (express or inferred) | Not required before you send, but you must stop once someone opts out |
| Sender identification | Must clearly and accurately identify the sender and give contact details | "From", reply address, and routing details must be accurate. The email must include a valid physical postal address |
| Subject lines and content | Must not be misleading (Australian Consumer Law also applies) | Must not be deceptive |
| Unsubscribe | Working unsubscribe facility, honoured within 5 working days, and working for at least 30 days after sending | Working opt-out, honoured within 10 business days, and working for at least 30 days after sending |
| Our default | Confirmed opt-in for every Member, in every country | Same |

Our confirmed opt-in default satisfies the consent rule in both countries. It does not satisfy every other rule. Sender identification, content, subject lines, and other laws still apply to every Campaign, and you remain responsible for them.

### 9.3 Service Messages are not for marketing

We send Service Messages such as one-time login codes and confirmation emails. These are not Marketing Emails. Clients must not use them to promote anything. We may send a Member a Service Message about their card or your program, for example that a program is closing, even if the Member has unsubscribed from marketing.

### 9.4 Links

Every link in a Campaign goes through a tracked redirect on our domain. A harmful link can damage our domain and every Client's deliverability. Links must go to lawful, safe destinations that you control or are entitled to link to. You must not use link shorteners, redirect chains, or destinations that download files. We may scan links, and we may disable any link or destination we consider risky.

### 9.5 Frequency and volume

Send at a reasonable frequency. We may set limits on volume and frequency, including the number of Campaigns per week, to protect Members and our sending reputation.

### 9.6 Stick to the provided templates

You must send Campaigns using the templates and tools in the dashboard. You must not edit, inject, or replace the underlying template HTML, send content outside the campaign builder, or try to make a Campaign look different from what the tool generates. The templates are how we keep every Campaign compliant, and working around them defeats that.

## 10. Prohibited Conduct and System Interference

You must not:

- import, upload, or send to a purchased, rented, scraped, or otherwise third-party-sourced contact list
- send to anyone who hasn't confirmed marketing consent, or who has unsubscribed, bounced, or complained
- disguise or falsify your identity, sender address, or subject line
- send unsolicited bulk email unrelated to a Member's membership in your program
- send viruses, malware, or phishing content through Campaigns or uploaded content
- edit, inject, or send Campaign content using code or HTML outside our templates and tools
- access, modify, or interfere with our scheduled sending system, including cron jobs, APIs, or any other backend infrastructure
- scan, probe, or test our security without our written permission
- use bots, scrapers, or automated tools to access the Platform, other than tools we provide
- get around a suspension or limit, including by opening another account
- let people outside your business use your account or login

If you find a security weakness, report it to support@loyalties.marketing with "Security" in the subject line. Give us reasonable time to fix it before you tell anyone else.

A breach of this section is treated as serious. See Section 17.

## 11. Looking After Member Data

Clients can see information about their Members in the dashboard. That information belongs to your Members and you must handle it carefully. You must:

- use Members' details only to run your loyalty program and to send marketing they agreed to
- not sell, rent, share, or swap Members' details
- not upload Members' details to advertising platforms, data brokers, or other marketing tools, or combine them with other lists to profile Members
- not contact Members for reasons unrelated to your program, including personal reasons
- give staff access only when they need it, use individual logins, never share passwords, and remove access when someone leaves
- keep any export or copy of Member data secure, and delete it when you no longer need it
- tell Members how you use their information, including in your own privacy notice where the law requires
- help us respond to Member requests to access, correct, or delete their information
- treat any request to stop receiving emails as an unsubscribe. If a Member asks you directly, tell us at support@loyalties.marketing and we will action it straight away
- tell us at support@loyalties.marketing as soon as you become aware, and within 48 hours, if you suspect unauthorised access to your account or to Member data

## 12. Conduct Standards for Members

When using a loyalty program on Loyalties Marketing, you must not:

- join if you are under 18
- provide false identity or email details, or sign up someone else's email address
- share your one-time login code or let someone else access your membership
- abuse stamp or NFC systems, including by cloning devices, creating multiple accounts, using scripts or automation, or claiming stamps you didn't earn
- sell, trade, or transfer stamps or rewards unless the Client allows it
- harass venue staff, our support team, or other users
- make reports in bad faith, for example to harm a business

Reports made in good faith are always welcome, even if we find no breach.

## 13. Intellectual Property and Takedowns

You must own or have permission to use everything you upload or send, including logos, photos, text, music, and fonts. Photos of identifiable people need those people's permission. Don't use another business's trademarks or brand assets in a way that suggests endorsement.

If you believe Client Content infringes your rights, email support@loyalties.marketing with "Copyright" in the subject line. Tell us:

- who you are and how to contact you
- what work is affected and where you own it
- where the content appears on the Platform
- why you believe it infringes
- that you are the owner or are authorised to act for the owner

We may remove or disable the content while we look into it, and we may tell the Client. We may suspend Clients who repeatedly infringe. If you are a Client and believe we removed content by mistake, reply with your reasons and we will review it.

## 14. Reporting a Concern

Anyone can report a concern at **loyalties.marketing/abuse**. Every Campaign also includes a "Report this email" link in its footer, which opens the report form with the Campaign already filled in.

The report form has a dropdown so you can tell us what kind of concern it is:

- Spam or consent issue, including "I never signed up"
- Harmful or unlawful content
- Misleading or unfair promotion
- Prize not provided
- Reward not honoured by a venue
- A Member who appears to be under 18
- Copyright or intellectual property
- Security issue or suspected phishing
- Something else

You don't need to be a Member, and you don't need to know any email address. You can report anonymously, but then we can't follow up with you, so we may only be able to act on what the report tells us. It helps to include the venue name, what the content or email was, and when you received it.

We won't give your name or email address to the Client without your permission, unless the law requires it. You won't be penalised for a good-faith report, even if we find no breach.

If someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services first (000 in Australia, 911 in the United States).

You can also complain to a regulator at any time, for example the Australian Communications and Media Authority (acma.gov.au) about spam, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) about privacy, the US Federal Trade Commission about email or promotions, or your state or territory consumer affairs regulator about a program or promotion.

## 15. What Happens After You Report

We aim to:

- acknowledge your report within 3 business days
- review urgent reports, which means child safety, threats of violence, phishing or malware, and self-harm, within 1 business day
- review the content or conduct against this Policy, and contact you if we need more information
- contact the Client and give them up to 5 business days to respond, unless the report needs immediate action
- tell you the outcome where we are able to. We may not be able to share details of action taken against a Client
- look at the outcome again if you reply to the ticket and disagree

These are targets, not guarantees.

## 16. Monitoring and Automatic Action

We may, but are not required to, monitor sending patterns, scan Campaign content and links, and review Campaigns. Every Client shares our sending reputation, so one Client's poor sending can affect all the others. That is why we act quickly. We may:

- review your sending if your spam complaint rate goes above 0.1% or your bounce rate goes above 2%
- pause your sending automatically if your spam complaint rate goes above 0.3% or your bounce rate goes above 4%
- hold a Campaign for review before it sends, including your first Campaign
- throttle, delay, or block sends, links, or content that look risky
- review every individual spam complaint
- keep records of reports, complaints, and sending activity so we can enforce this Policy and respond to regulators

We measure these rates over a rolling 30 days, once you have sent enough emails for the rate to be meaningful.

## 17. Enforcement and Fair Process

What happens next depends on how serious the breach is.

| Severity | Example | Typical response |
|---|---|---|
| Minor, first instance | A broken or malformed Campaign sent unintentionally | Warning, with a required fix before sending again |
| Moderate | Repeated broken sends, borderline content in a template field, complaint or bounce rates above our review levels | Sending paused until reviewed |
| Serious | Sending to unconfirmed contacts, trying to edit template HTML or bypass consent controls, hate speech, sexual content, fraud, knowingly enrolling people under 18 | Immediate suspension of the account or the affected feature |
| Unlawful | Unauthorised access to our systems or cron jobs, or any conduct that breaks the law | Account termination, and reporting to the relevant authorities |

We may also remove or quarantine content, deactivate a Member's card, opt Members out of marketing, and cooperate with regulators and law enforcement.

**Fair process.** We want to be fair to Clients as well as Members.

- Where the risk is not immediate, we will tell you what we found and why it matters, and give you 5 business days to respond before we act.
- Where the risk is immediate, such as a compromised account, a complaint spike, or serious harm, we may act first. We will tell you as soon as practical afterward and explain why. We will review our decision within 3 business days of your response.
- If you fix the problem and we are satisfied, we will restore your access.
- If we end your account for breach, you can export your data for 30 days afterward, unless the law prevents it or the breach involved unlawful conduct.
- If we close a Client's account, Members' cards for that venue may stop working. We will take reasonable steps to tell Members.
- We may close accounts opened to get around an enforcement action.

For refunds after enforcement, see Section 2 of our Marketing Platform Policy.
